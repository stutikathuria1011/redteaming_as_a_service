- **jbb**: Chao et al., JailbreakBench: An Open Robustness Benchmark for Jailbreaking LLMs, arXiv:2404.01318 (2024)
- **strongreject**: Souly et al., A StrongREJECT for Empty Jailbreaks, arXiv:2402.10260 (2024)
